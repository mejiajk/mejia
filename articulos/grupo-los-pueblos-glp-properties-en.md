## How to Verify the Company

We do not ask you to simply trust us; we ask you to check. Before buying from any developer, including us, follow these steps.

1. **Legal existence.** Request the legal name and check with Panama's Public Registry that the company is registered and active, who its representatives are, and whether it has liens or encumbrances.
2. **Tax ID.** Confirm the company's RUC and make sure it matches the one in the purchase promise agreement and in your payment receipts.
3. **Land ownership.** Ask for the property (finca) number where the project is built and verify in the Public Registry whose name it is under and whether it carries mortgages.
4. **Permits.** Request the construction and preliminary project permits and confirm with the competent authority that they are valid.
5. **Where your payments go.** Ask which account or structure (for example, a trust) receives your deposits and what protection you have if the project is delayed.
6. **Track record.** Visit delivered projects and speak with owners.
7. **Legal review.** Have an independent lawyer review the contract before you sign.
