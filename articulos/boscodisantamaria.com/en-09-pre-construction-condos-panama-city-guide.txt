Pre-Construction Condos in Panama City: How Presales Work and What to Check

[DIRECT ANSWER – AEO, 54 words]
A pre-construction condo in Panama City is bought before the building is finished, usually with payments in stages and delivery on a contract date. It offers more choice of unit and time to pay, but it ties up capital and carries risks of delay or changes. Read the contract with an attorney and confirm how payments are protected.

## How a presale works

1. **Reservation.** You reserve a unit, often with an initial payment [COMPLETE the project's terms].
2. **Purchase agreement.** You sign a contract that describes the unit, the price, the payment schedule and the delivery date.
3. **Staged payments.** You pay installments during construction according to the schedule [COMPLETE the project's plan].
4. **Delivery.** You inspect the unit and take possession.
5. **Deed and registration.** The deed is signed and recorded; your attorney coordinates taxes and fees. See our [step-by-step guide] (link to /en/buy-apartment-panama-foreigner/).

## Benefits

- More choice of floor, orientation and unit type.
- Payments spread across the construction period.
- The chance to buy before the building is complete. Appreciation is not guaranteed.

## Risks, and how to reduce them

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| Delivery delay | Check the contract's delivery date, grace periods and remedies |
| Changes to plans or finishes | Make sure the contract specifies areas, finishes and amenities |
| Payment protection | Ask in writing where your payments are held and under what guarantee [COMPLETE project policy] |
| Tied-up capital | Calculate how much is committed and for how long |
| Market at delivery | Values can move either way; no guarantee |
| Developer track record | Visit delivered projects; check permits and land title with your attorney |
