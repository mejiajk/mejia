Golf Course Condos in Panama: A Buyer's Checklist

[DIRECT ANSWER – AEO, 55 words]
A golf course condo in Panama is an apartment in or next to a golf community. Before buying, check whether golf membership is included or separate, the monthly fees, rules on renting the unit, which amenities are open today versus planned, the view's orientation, and the delivery date if the project is under construction.

## The seven checks before you buy

1. **Membership.** Is golf access included with the unit, or is membership sold separately? Ask for the cost, the transfer rules and whether it can be resold with the apartment. Do not rely on third-party numbers: get them from the club in writing.
2. **Fees.** Ask for the monthly maintenance for the building and any community fee. Ask what each covers.
3. **Rental rules.** If you may rent the unit, ask whether short-stay rentals are allowed.
4. **Amenities.** Separate what is operating today from what is planned. [COMPLETE for each community you cover.]
5. **View and orientation.** A "golf view" depends on the floor and the side of the building. Ask for the exact unit's orientation and, if possible, visit.
6. **Status and delivery.** Under construction or ready? If under construction, get the delivery date and the delay terms in the contract.
7. **Legal review.** Have a Panamanian attorney review the contract, title and permits. See our [step-by-step guide for foreign buyers] (link to /en/buy-apartment-panama-foreigner/).

## Under construction or ready?

An off-plan unit can offer more choice and staged payments, but you wait for delivery and carry the risk of delays. A finished unit lets you inspect what you buy. Read [how pre-construction purchases work] (link to /en/pre-construction-condos-panama-city-guide/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are golf condos in Panama a good investment?
There is no general answer, and returns are not guaranteed. They depend on purchase price, fees, occupancy and resale demand. Run the numbers for the specific unit.

### Can foreigners buy golf course condos in Panama?
In general foreigners can buy property in Panama, with restrictions in specific zones. Confirm your case with an attorney.

### Is the golf membership included?
It depends on the community and the unit. Ask for it in writing before you sign.

### Which Panamanian golf community is best?
It depends on your priorities: location, budget, product and membership terms. Use the seven checks above to compare.
