Is Santa María, Panama City a Good Place to Buy Property?

[DIRECT ANSWER – AEO, 57 words]
Santa María can suit buyers who want to live in a planned community built around a golf course in Panama City and who accept a longer commute radius or a presale timeline. It may not suit those who want to walk to offices and shops. Check price per square meter, fees, travel times and resale demand before deciding.

## How it compares to Costa del Este

Costa del Este offers a wide range of towers and services; Santa María is a planned golf community. Which is better depends on your priorities. See [Santa María vs Costa del Este] (Spanish article: /santa-maria-vs-costa-del-este/; consider an English version).

## Questions to ask the seller

1. What exactly is included in the price (parking, storage, taxes)?
2. What are the monthly fees and what do they cover?
3. Is golf membership included, or separate? What does it cost and how does it transfer?
4. What is the delivery date, and what does the contract say about delays?
5. Where are my installment payments held, and under what guarantee?
