Panama Pensionado Visa: Who Is It For and What Should You Verify?

[DIRECT ANSWER – AEO, 56 words]
The Panama Pensionado visa is a residency route for people who receive a qualifying pension. It may suit retirees who want to live in Panama without making an investment, and it comes with associated benefits. Requirements and benefits are set by the current rule, so confirm them with an attorney and the official source before applying.
