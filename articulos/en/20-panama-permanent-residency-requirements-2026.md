# Panama Permanent Residency Requirements for 2026

Requirements depend on which route you choose. This overview covers what is common and what is specific.

> Amounts and eligibility lists change by decree. Confirm with immigration authorities or a licensed attorney.

## Common Requirements

- Valid passport.
- Apostilled police record(s).
- Medical certificate from Panama.
- Application filed by a licensed Panamanian attorney.
- Proof supporting the specific category.
- Supporting civil documents for dependents.

## Requirements by Route

### Pensionado
Lifetime pension letter; historically a US$1,000 monthly minimum plus an amount per dependent.

### Friendly Nations
Eligible nationality, an economic or professional tie, and, per some sources, a bank deposit around US$5,000.

### Qualified Investor
An approved investment with reported thresholds of US$300,000–500,000 or more, and proof of lawful funds.

## Process Summary

1. Select the category.
2. Prepare and apostille documents.
3. Retain an attorney.
4. Visit Panama for signing and medical.
5. Filing, review and resolution.

See our breakdowns of costs and processing time. Estimates range from weeks to a few months depending on the route.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Using outdated lists or figures.
- Skipping legal review.
- Failing to document funds.

## FAQ

### Is a Panamanian permanent residency the same as citizenship?
No. Citizenship is a separate application after the legal period.

### Can family be included?
Usually yes, with extra documents.
