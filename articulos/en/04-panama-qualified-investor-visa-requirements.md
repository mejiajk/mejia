# Panama Qualified Investor Visa: Requirements Explained

The Qualified Investor route grants permanent residency in exchange for an approved investment. According to sources, it was introduced by Executive Decree 722 in October 2020.

## Who It Suits

Investors who want permanent residency without a job offer or pension, and who can commit capital.

## Eligible Investments

Firms describe options such as:

- **Real estate** meeting the minimum value.
- **Securities** and other authorized financial instruments.
- **Fixed-term deposits** in local banks.

## Key Requirements

1. Proof of the investment and lawful source of funds.
2. Apostilled police record.
3. Medical certificate.
4. Filing through a licensed Panamanian attorney.

## Reported Minimums

Figures vary:

- Some sources: **US$300,000** for qualifying real estate.
- Others: **US$500,000** or a US$500,000–750,000 range.
- One source says a 2024 decree lowered certain real estate thresholds.

**Confirm the current figure in writing** before committing funds.

## Why Investors Choose It

- Described as among the fastest residency routes.
- Dependents can be included.
- Eligible for citizenship after the legal waiting period.

## Things to Check

- Source-of-funds compliance at your bank.
- Closing costs, taxes and fees.
- Whether you must keep the asset to keep status.

## FAQ

### Do I need to visit Panama?
Sources differ, but expect at least one trip.

### How fast is it?
One firm claims 30–45 business days. Treat as marketing.
