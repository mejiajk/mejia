# Panama Friendly Nations Visa: Requirements for 2026

The Friendly Nations Visa is one of the most searched routes to Panamanian residency. It is designed for nationals of countries with strong professional, economic and investment ties to Panama.

## What Is the Friendly Nations Visa?

The category was created by Executive Decree 416 of 2012 and expanded by Decree 226 of 2021. It lets qualifying nationals apply for residency by demonstrating a real link to Panama.

## Core Requirements

1. **Age:** 18 or older.
2. **Nationality:** citizen of a country on the current list.
3. **Economic or professional tie:** a Panamanian operating company, job offer, investment, or real estate purchase.
4. **Bank account:** several sources mention a deposit of around US$5,000. Confirm the current amount.
5. **Clean police record,** apostilled.
6. **Medical certificate** from Panama.
7. **A licensed Panamanian attorney** to file.

## Is It Temporary or Permanent?

Sources conflict. One says holders get a two-year legal residence first and can apply for permanent status afterward; others describe it as permanent. Ask your attorney for written confirmation.

## Typical Costs

Expect government fees, attorney fees, apostille and translation costs, plus whatever it takes to build the tie (company setup or property). A forum cites government fees above US$500 for treaty countries and above US$1,000 otherwise; that is user-reported, not official.

## Pros and Limits

**Pros:** flexible ways to show a tie, family can be included, path to permanent residency.
**Limits:** depends on country eligibility and on proving a genuine connection.

## FAQ

### Can I qualify without buying property?
Sources indicate that a company or job offer can also work.

### How long does it take?
Document prep of 2–4 weeks and a visit of about five business days are commonly cited, plus agency processing time.
