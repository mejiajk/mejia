# Panama Citizenship After 5 Years of Residency

Several sources say permanent residents can apply for Panamanian citizenship after five years of legal residency. Here is what that involves.

## The General Rule

Five years of legal residency is the commonly cited baseline. The Constitution provides shorter periods for certain nationals, such as some Spanish and Latin American citizens. Ask your attorney which period applies to you.

## Typical Requirements

1. Valid permanent residency and the required residence period.
2. Clean criminal record.
3. Basic Spanish.
4. Knowledge of Panamanian history, geography and civics, where required.
5. Lawful means of support.
6. Filing through an attorney.

## Physical Presence

Some guides say maintaining residency requires visiting only periodically (for example once every two years). Naturalization may require effective residence. Verify how time is counted.

## Second Passport Considerations

- Check whether your home country allows dual citizenship.
- Review tax consequences; citizenship doesn't automatically change tax residency.
- Understand obligations that come with nationality.

## Does Investing Shorten the Wait?

Not automatically. A residency-by-investment route gives residency, not citizenship.

## Planning Ahead

Keep records of entries and exits, renew your card on time and avoid status issues.

## FAQ

### Do I need to renounce my nationality?
That depends on both countries' laws. Check both.

### Can I lose residency before naturalizing?
Yes, if conditions are not met.
