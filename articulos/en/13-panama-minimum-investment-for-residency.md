# Panama Minimum Investment for Residency

The most common question: how much do you actually need to invest? The honest answer is that it depends on the program, the asset type and the date, and sources don't agree.

## What Sources Report

| Source type | Figure cited | Asset |
|---|---|---|
| GLP-associated site | US$300,000 | Real estate |
| Law firms | US$500,000 | Commercial or real estate |
| Other firms | US$500,000–750,000 | Varies by asset |
| 2024 mention | Some real estate thresholds lowered | Real estate |

These are commercial sources. Verify before relying on any number.

## Why the Numbers Differ

- Different asset categories.
- Decree changes over time.
- Outdated articles that keep circulating.

## Lower-Cost Alternatives

- **Friendly Nations:** requires an economic tie; sources mention bank accounts from US$5,000.
- **Pensionado:** no investment, but a lifetime pension is needed.

## How to Confirm the Current Minimum

1. Ask your attorney for the decree and article in force.
2. Get the figure and date in writing.
3. Cross-check with the immigration authority.

## Costs Not Counted as Investment

Attorney fees, government fees, transfer taxes and closing costs.

## FAQ

### Can I combine multiple properties?
Depends on the regulation; ask before buying.

### Can I use a mortgage?
Some sources say financing may be possible; confirm how it counts toward the minimum.
