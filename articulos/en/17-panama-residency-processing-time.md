# Panama Residency Processing Time: What to Expect

Timelines vary by category, completeness of documents and the agency's workload. Here are the phases and reported estimates.

## Phases

| Phase | What happens | Reported estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Apostilles and document gathering at home | 2–4 weeks |
| Trip to Panama | Powers of attorney, medical, bank | About 5 business days |
| Filing | Attorney files with immigration | A few days |
| Review | Immigration examines the case | Variable |
| Resolution & card | Approval and ID issuance | Variable |

## Marketing Claims vs. Reality

One investor-program site claims most applications are approved within 30–45 business days. Treat this as an optimistic marketing figure, not a guarantee.

## What Speeds Things Up

- Complete, apostilled, current documents.
- The right visa category from the start.
- An attorney who responds quickly to requests.
- Bank account or investment already in place.

## What Slows Things Down

- Missing or expired apostilles.
- Inconsistencies between documents.
- Switching categories mid-process.
- Additional requests from immigration.

## By Category

- **Pensionado and Qualified Investor:** typically described among the faster routes.
- **Friendly Nations:** may include a temporary stage, per some sources; confirm.

## FAQ

### Can I leave Panama while waiting?
Ask your attorney; you may need to appear for certain steps.

### When do I get the card?
After approval and final procedures, which can require a visit.
