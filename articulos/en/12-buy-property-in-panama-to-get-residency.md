# Buy Property in Panama to Get Residency

Yes, property can lead to residency, but only through specific programs and above certain values.

## Which Programs Accept Property?

1. **Qualified Investor:** real estate meeting the minimum value gives permanent residency. Reported minimums vary: US$300,000 to US$500,000 or more.
2. **Friendly Nations:** one source says personal property ownership can establish the economic tie for eligible nationalities.

## Step-by-Step Buying Process

1. Set budget and goal (home, rental, investment).
2. Hire an attorney before reserving.
3. Verify title and encumbrances.
4. Sign a promissory contract and pay the deposit.
5. Close at a notary and register the deed.
6. File the residency application.

## Costs Beyond the Price

- Transfer and registry taxes.
- Notary and legal fees.
- HOA or maintenance fees.
- Annual property tax, where applicable.

## What Buyers Often Miss

- The value that counts for residency may differ from the sale price.
- Financing may or may not count fully toward the minimum.
- Off-plan purchases carry delivery risk.

## Neighborhood Selection

Consider commute, services, rental demand and building quality. Spend time on the ground before deciding.

## FAQ

### Do I need to live in the property?
Not necessarily; confirm program rules.

### Can I sell later and keep residency?
Ask your attorney whether holding the asset is a condition.
