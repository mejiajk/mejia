# Panama Residency for UK Citizens: Complete Guide

British retirees, entrepreneurs and remote workers are increasingly considering Panama. Here is how residency works for UK passport holders.

## Routes to Consider

- **Friendly Nations Visa:** the UK is generally mentioned among eligible countries; confirm the current list. You need an economic or professional tie.
- **Pensionado:** if you receive a lifetime pension (State Pension or occupational), you may qualify, subject to the minimum.
- **Qualified Investor:** residency through an approved investment (reported US$300,000–500,000 or more).

## Documents

- Valid UK passport.
- ACRO or equivalent police certificate, apostilled.
- Marriage and birth certificates for dependents, apostilled.
- Panama medical certificate.

The UK apostille is issued by the relevant government office; check processing times.

## Tax and Pension Notes

- Becoming non-resident for UK tax requires meeting the statutory residence test, not just moving.
- Overseas State Pension uprating can depend on the destination country.
- Consider UK-Panama tax treatment of pensions and property income.

Take advice from a UK tax adviser before relocating.

## Process

1. Select a category.
2. Gather and apostille documents.
3. Hire a licensed Panamanian attorney.
4. Visit Panama for signatures and medical.
5. Your attorney files and tracks the case.

Yes, as a dependent in most routes.
