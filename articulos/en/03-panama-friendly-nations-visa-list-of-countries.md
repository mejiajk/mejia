# Panama Friendly Nations Visa: Which Countries Qualify?

Before planning anything, find out whether your nationality is eligible. This page explains how to check and why published lists differ.

> **We do not publish a fixed list.** It changes by decree, and sources report anywhere from about 50 to 53 countries. Always confirm against the official text.

## Why Lists Disagree

- Decrees expand or modify eligibility over time.
- Many blogs copy older lists.
- Some sources count differently (e.g., territories or special cases).

## Countries Frequently Mentioned

Third-party guides often include:

- **Americas:** United States, Canada, Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Brazil.
- **Europe:** Spain and other EU members, the United Kingdom, Switzerland.
- **Asia-Pacific:** Australia, Japan, South Korea, Singapore and others.

Appearing in a blog is not official confirmation.

## How to Verify

1. Check the latest decree in Panama's Official Gazette.
2. Review the National Immigration Service website.
3. Ask a licensed attorney for a dated written confirmation.

## If Your Country Isn't Listed

Look at other routes: Pensionado, Qualified Investor, work or business-based categories.

## Does Being Eligible Guarantee Approval?

No. You still need a genuine economic or professional tie and complete documents.

## FAQ

### How often does the list change?
It can change whenever a new decree is issued, so check the date of any source.

### Can I rely on a law firm's list?
Use it as a starting point and confirm independently.
