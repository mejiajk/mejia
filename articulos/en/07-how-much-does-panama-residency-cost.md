# How Much Does Panama Residency Cost?

There is no single price. Your total depends on the visa, whether you must invest, and your attorney's fees.

> We avoid fixed price tables because they change. Ask for a detailed written quote.

## What You'll Pay For

### 1. Government Fees
A forum cites fees above US$500 for treaty countries and above US$1,000 otherwise. That is user-reported, not official.

### 2. Attorney Fees
These vary with complexity and the number of applicants. Request a contract that states scope, timeline and included expenses.

### 3. Documents
Apostilles, official translations, police records and the medical certificate.

### 4. Economic Tie or Investment
- **Friendly Nations:** bank account (sources mention US$5,000), company or property.
- **Pensionado:** no investment, but a lifetime pension is required.
- **Qualified Investor:** real estate or financial investment, with reported thresholds of US$300,000–500,000 or more.

### 5. Travel
At least one short stay for signing and procedures.

## Comparison

| Visa | Investment? | Main cost |
|---|---|---|
| Pensionado | No | Fees + legal |
| Friendly Nations | Moderate | Tie + fees + legal |
| Qualified Investor | High | The investment + closing costs |

## Avoiding Surprises

- Compare at least two quotes.
- Be wary of "all-inclusive" offers without detail.
- Ask what happens if immigration requests extra documents.

## FAQ

### Are there ongoing costs?
Card renewal and, depending on your setup, company or property upkeep.

### Do I pay everything upfront?
Negotiate milestone payments.
