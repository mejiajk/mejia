# Panama Residency for Canadians: Options and Steps

Many Canadians look to Panama for warmer weather, lower living costs in some areas, and a dollarized economy.

## Residency Routes

- **Friendly Nations Visa:** Canada is commonly listed among eligible countries. Verify the current list. An economic tie is required.
- **Pensionado:** for those with a qualifying lifetime pension, such as CPP, OAS or an employer plan.
- **Qualified Investor:** based on investment, not nationality.

## Documents

- Valid Canadian passport.
- RCMP criminal record check, authenticated/apostilled as currently required.
- Birth and marriage certificates for dependents.
- Medical certificate from Panama.

Check Canada's current document legalization procedure, which can differ from standard apostille practice.

## Taxes and Provincial Considerations

Panama's territorial system doesn't automatically end Canadian tax residency. Leaving Canada for tax purposes can involve departure tax and ties tests, plus provincial health coverage rules. Get Canadian tax advice before moving.

## Healthcare

Review insurance and portability of coverage. Provincial plans usually have time-away limits.

## Steps

1. Pick your category.
2. Obtain and authenticate documents.
3. Retain a Panamanian attorney.
4. Travel to Panama for the medical exam and signatures.
5. Your attorney files and follows up.

## FAQ

### Can I be a snowbird without residency?
Tourist stays are limited; residency gives you a stable legal status.

### Can I keep Canadian citizenship?
Yes. Panama residency doesn't affect citizenship.
