# Panama Pensionado Visa: Requirements and Benefits

The Pensionado program is among Panama's best-known residency routes. Sources describe it as granting permanent residency to retirees with guaranteed lifetime pensions.

## Core Requirements

1. **Lifetime pension:** historically a minimum of about US$1,000 per month, with an extra amount per dependent. Confirm current figures.
2. **Official pension letter** from the paying entity.
3. **Apostilled police record.**
4. **Medical certificate** issued in Panama.
5. **A licensed Panamanian attorney** to file.

## Which Pensions Qualify?

Social security and lifetime retirement plans typically work. Rental or investment income generally does not count as a pension.

## Benefits

Sources mention discounts on services, transport, entertainment and healthcare. Check the current official list, since it can change.

## Step by Step

1. Get your pension letter.
2. Legalize documents at home.
3. Travel to Panama for the medical exam and signatures.
4. Your attorney files and follows up.
