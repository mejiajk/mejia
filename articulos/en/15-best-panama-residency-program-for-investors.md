# Best Panama Residency Program for Investors

The "best" program depends on your capital, nationality and goals. Here is a framework to compare options.

## The Contenders

### Qualified Investor
- **Best for:** investors wanting permanent residency based on capital.
- **Capital:** reported US$300,000–500,000+ (verify).
- **Speed:** widely described as among the fastest.

### Friendly Nations
- **Best for:** nationals of eligible countries wanting a lower entry cost.
- **Capital:** economic tie such as a company or property; sources mention bank deposits from US$5,000.
- **Caveat:** status (temporary vs. permanent) is described inconsistently.

### Pensionado
- **Best for:** retirees with a pension, not investors per se.

## Side-by-Side

| Factor | Qualified Investor | Friendly Nations |
|---|---|---|
| Based on | Investment | Nationality + tie |
| Capital | Higher | Lower (varies) |
| Nationality limits | None | Country list |
| Status | Permanent | Verify |

## How to Decide

1. Is your country on the Friendly Nations list?
2. How much capital are you ready to commit and lock in?
3. Do you want a property you can use or rent?
4. How important is speed and permanence?

## Hidden Considerations

- Source-of-funds checks.
- Exit options for the asset.
- Tax implications at home.

## FAQ

### Can I start with Friendly Nations and invest later?
Possibly. Ask your attorney about sequencing.

### Does investing speed up citizenship?
Not automatically; residency and nationality are different.
