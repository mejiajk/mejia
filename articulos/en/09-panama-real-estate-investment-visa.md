# Panama Real Estate Investment Visa Explained

Buying property is one of the most direct ways to secure Panamanian residency. But not every purchase qualifies, and the minimum depends on the program.

## Two Routes That Involve Property

### Qualified Investor
Gives permanent residency for approved investments, including qualifying real estate. Reported minimums range from **US$300,000** to **US$500,000 or more**, depending on source and date.

### Friendly Nations (economic tie)
One source says buying a property in your own name can count as the economic tie, even with bank financing. This applies only to eligible nationalities.

## Due Diligence Checklist

1. **Title search** at the Public Registry for liens.
2. **Developer track record,** permits and construction status.
3. **Independent appraisal.**
4. **Closing costs:** transfer taxes, notary and attorney fees.
5. **Ongoing costs:** property tax, HOA/maintenance fees.

## New Build vs. Resale

| | New build | Resale |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Variable timeline | Immediate |
| Risk | Delays, project changes | Physical condition |
| Payment | Staged | Cash or mortgage |

## Rental Strategy

If you plan to rent, study demand by neighborhood and check building rules on short-term rentals.

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming any purchase grants residency.
- Not confirming the threshold at filing time.
- Signing reservation agreements without legal review.

## FAQ

### Can foreigners own property in Panama?
Generally yes, with some restrictions in border or protected zones. Confirm with your attorney.

### How quickly does residency follow the purchase?
One firm cites 30–45 business days; treat as marketing.
