# Friendly Nations vs Qualified Investor Visa in Panama

Both are popular routes, but they work differently. Here is a clear comparison.

## Quick Comparison

| | Friendly Nations | Qualified Investor |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Decree 416 (2012), expanded by Decree 226 (2021) | Decree 722 (2020), per sources |
| Eligibility | Nationals of listed countries | Any nationality |
| Requirement | Economic/professional tie | Approved investment |
| Capital | Lower, varies | Higher (reported US$300,000–500,000+) |
| Status | Temporary then permanent, per one source; others say permanent | Permanent |
| Speed | Varies | Described as among the fastest |

## When Friendly Nations Fits

- Your nationality is on the list.
- You prefer a lower upfront commitment.
- You can build a genuine link via a company, job or property.

## When Qualified Investor Fits

- Your nationality isn't on the list.
- You want permanent status from the outset.
- You plan to invest in real estate or financial assets anyway.

## Risks and Trade-offs

**Friendly Nations:** list changes, and proof of a real tie is essential.
**Qualified Investor:** larger capital at stake, liquidity and source-of-funds scrutiny.

## Can You Combine Them?

They are separate categories. Ask your attorney if one can lead to another in your situation.

## FAQ

### Which is cheaper?
Typically Friendly Nations, but total cost depends on how you build the tie.

### Which is faster?
Sources often cite the investor route as faster; confirm with current processing times.
