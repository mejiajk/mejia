# Panama Immigration Lawyer Cost and How to Choose

Sources state that residency applications in Panama must be filed by a licensed attorney. That makes your choice of lawyer one of the most important decisions.

## What Drives the Cost

- **Visa category:** investor cases involve more verification than a simple pension case.
- **Number of applicants:** each dependent adds work.
- **Extra services:** company formation, bank account assistance, property due diligence.
- **Document handling:** apostilles, translations, courier.

We do not list fixed fees, which vary by firm and change over time. Ask for a written quote.

## What Should Be Included

- Category assessment.
- Document review and checklist.
- Filing and responses to immigration requests.
- Status updates and a timeline.

## Questions to Ask

1. Which category do you recommend and why?
2. What is the fee, and what is excluded?
3. How many trips to Panama will I need?
4. How do you handle extra document requests?
5. How are payments structured?

## Red Flags

- "Guaranteed approval."
- Full payment upfront with no contract.
- Vague answers about the legal basis.
- Pressure to decide immediately.

## Firms You'll See in Search

Firms with visible presence include NDM, Kraemer Law and Relofirm, among others. Mention is not endorsement; evaluate with the criteria above.

## FAQ

### Can I hire remotely?
Many firms work with a notarized power of attorney.

### Can I switch lawyers mid-process?
Possible, but review your contract and file handover.
