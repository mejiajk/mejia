# Panama Residency Tax Benefits: Understanding Territorial Taxation

Panama's territorial tax system is a major draw. But it is often oversimplified, so here is a balanced view.

> General information only. Tax outcomes depend on your home country. Consult a qualified adviser.

## The Territorial Principle

Broadly, Panama taxes income generated within Panama. Foreign-source income generally isn't taxed locally. Exceptions and conditions apply.

## What May Be Taxed in Panama

- Income from work or business carried out in Panama.
- Rental income from Panamanian property.
- Property-related taxes and transfer taxes.

## What Residency Does Not Do

- **US citizens** are taxed on worldwide income regardless of residence.
- Many countries tax based on **tax residency**, which doesn't change automatically when you get a Panamanian visa.
- Reporting rules for foreign accounts and assets may still apply.

## Practical Steps

1. Determine your current tax residency and how it ends in your country.
2. Check tax treaties for double-taxation relief.
3. Track days of presence and personal and economic ties.
4. Keep documentation.

## Investors

Consider transfer taxes, annual property taxes and rental income taxation, and how your home country treats foreign property income.

## FAQ

### Is Panama a tax haven?
That label oversimplifies a system with compliance and transparency obligations.

### Do I need a Panamanian tax ID?
Often yes for local activity, property or banking; ask your adviser.
