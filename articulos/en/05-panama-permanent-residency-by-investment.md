# Panama Permanent Residency by Investment

Panama's investor residency lets qualifying applicants and their families obtain permanent status through approved capital placements. Here is how it works in practice.

## How the Program Works

You invest a minimum amount in an approved asset, document the funds, and file through a Panamanian attorney. On approval, you receive permanent residency.

## Investment Types

- Real estate
- Securities
- Fixed-term bank deposits

Not every purchase qualifies; the asset must meet the program's rules.

## Process

1. Define budget and objective.
2. Confirm threshold with your attorney.
3. Perform due diligence on the asset.
4. Close the investment and file the application.
5. Receive resolution and residency card.

## Risks

- Overpriced assets: get an independent appraisal.
- Title issues: run a Public Registry search.
- Policy changes: obtain dated legal advice.
- Timing promises: ask for estimates, not guarantees.

## After Approval

Review obligations such as renewing your card and keeping the investment if required. Sources say citizenship can be requested after five years of legal residency.

## FAQ

### Is it the same as a "golden visa"?
Media often use that label. The legal name is Qualified Investor.

### Can I rent out the property?
Often yes, but check the building's rules and tax treatment.
