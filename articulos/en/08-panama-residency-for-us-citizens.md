# Panama Residency for US Citizens: 2026 Guide

Panama is a popular destination for Americans thanks to its dollarized economy, direct flights and straightforward residency routes.

## Your Main Options

### Friendly Nations Visa
The United States appears on the list of eligible countries cited by most sources. Confirm the current list. You will need an economic tie: a company, job, investment or property.

### Pensionado
If you receive a lifetime pension, such as Social Security or a qualifying private pension, you may qualify.

### Qualified Investor
Independent of nationality; based on an approved investment (reported US$300,000–500,000 or more).

## Documents for Americans

- Valid US passport.
- FBI Identity History Summary, apostilled.
- Birth and marriage certificates, apostilled, if including family.
- Panama medical certificate.

## Taxes: What Doesn't Change

Panama taxes largely on a territorial basis, but **the United States taxes its citizens on worldwide income**, wherever they live. You will still file US returns and may need to report foreign accounts. Speak with a US tax professional.

## Banking in Panama

Banks apply compliance checks and will ask for source-of-funds documents. Prepare statements in advance.

## Process Overview

1. Choose a visa category.
2. Gather and apostille documents.
3. Hire a Panamanian attorney.
4. Travel to Panama for signatures and the medical exam.
5. Attorney files; you receive the resolution and card.

## FAQ

### Do Americans need a visa to enter Panama?
Check current entry rules for US passport holders before traveling.

### Can I keep my US residency or address?
Legal residency in Panama doesn't automatically change your US tax status.
