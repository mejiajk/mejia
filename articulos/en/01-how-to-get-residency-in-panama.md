# How to Get Residency in Panama: A 2026 Guide

Panama offers several residency routes, each aimed at a different profile: retirees, investors, and nationals of "friendly" countries. This guide explains how the process works and how to choose.

> **Verify before you act:** thresholds and country lists change by decree. Confirm current rules with Panama's National Immigration Service or a licensed Panamanian attorney.

## The Main Residency Routes

### Friendly Nations Visa
Created by Executive Decree 416 of 2012 and expanded by Decree 226 of 2021, it is open to nationals of eligible countries who show an economic or professional tie to Panama (a company, job, investment or property).

### Pensionado Visa
For retirees with a lifetime pension. Sources describe it as granting permanent residency from the start.

### Qualified Investor Program
Introduced by Executive Decree 722 in October 2020 according to sources, it grants permanent residency through an approved investment.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Choose your category** based on nationality, income and capital.
2. **Gather documents:** passport, apostilled police record, marriage or birth certificates for dependents.
3. **Hire a Panamanian attorney.** Sources state applications must be filed by a licensed lawyer.
4. **Visit Panama** to sign powers of attorney, complete a medical check and, where relevant, open a bank account or formalize the investment.
5. **Filing and follow-up:** your attorney files and answers any requests from immigration.

## How Long Does It Take?

Some firms mention 2–4 weeks of document preparation and about 5 business days in Panama, plus processing time. One investor-program site claims 30–45 business days; treat that as marketing, not a guarantee.

## Common Mistakes

- Relying on outdated country lists.
- Skipping apostilles.
- Paying intermediaries without a written contract.

## FAQ

### Can I apply without traveling to Panama?
Sources disagree. Much of the preparation can be done remotely, but at least one trip is generally needed.

### Can my family be included?
Yes, spouses and dependent children can usually be included.
