Beachfront Condos in Panama for Foreign Buyers: A Buyer's Checklist

[DIRECT ANSWER – AEO, 53 words]
Foreigners can generally buy property in Panama, including condos near the coast, with restrictions in specific zones. For a beachfront condo, confirm the legal status of the land and shoreline with an attorney, then check fees, short-term rental rules, delivery date, the developer's track record and how your payments are protected before signing.

DISCLOSURE (visible): Grupo Los Pueblos publishes this checklist and has projects on the coast. We give criteria so you can compare any project, including ours.

NOTICE (visible): Informational content, not legal or tax advice. Verify with a licensed Panamanian attorney.
