## Implementation notes

- **One page per intent.** Sales and rentals should not share a URL.
- **hreflang.** Link each EN page to its ES equivalent to avoid cannibalization.
- **Don't translate literally.** The EN audience is mostly foreign buyers and expats, so cover residency, financing and foreign ownership.
- **Schema.** Use `RealEstateListing` on each listing and `Offer` with price where prices are public.
- **CTAs.** Keep WhatsApp and tour booking visible on every transactional page.
