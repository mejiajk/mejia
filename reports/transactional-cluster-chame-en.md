## Prioritization

1. **First 6 and 5:** highest English volume, low local competition.
2. **Then 1, 4 and 8:** already ranking #2–5 on brand terms; they need a dedicated page and a buy CTA.
3. **Then 2, 3 and 7:** low volume, clear intent, almost no competition.

## Caveats

- Figures of 10/month mean "demand exists", not reliable traffic estimates.
- Clusters 1, 4 and 8 rely on brand terms: they convert well but add little new traffic. For new traffic, prioritize 5 and 6.
- US "panama beach …" queries excluded: they overlap with Panama City Beach, Florida.
- The site could not be crawled directly (blocked by the session proxy); on-page findings come from the DataForSEO API.
